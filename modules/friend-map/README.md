# The friend map module

The life archive's social layer, by the captain's call: the messages, calls and hangouts from his apps, everyone in them as people he names, tags and joins, and friend-map's page over them, in the console's Friend Map (`/life-archive/dev/friend-map`) and on its own at `/friend-map/`. LLM sessions are not its: they stay the core's (the event index, `core.sqlite`). The core's README says how it all works ("People", "Changing data from a chat", "The message archive", "Telegram, live", "WhatsApp, live", "This Mac's calls and Messages, every 15 minutes", "Exports from the Cua Space"), and how a module is laid out, found and switched (`docs/modules.md`).

It is the fifth module and the largest: until it, all of this was the core. It runs inside a life archive, never on its own. The core loads `module.ts` (its server half) and `viewer.tsx` (its page half), and the module imports the core through the SDK alone (`../../sdk/`).

- `module.ts`: its database, the archive (`server/db.ts`, opened by its own runner, `database.open`), read by the viewer's tables on a connection that refuses content (`server/sqlRead.ts`); its twelve connectors (`connectors/connectors.ts`), which the core's host runs as it runs its own, each import's events applied to the archive (`server/write.ts`) and what they touched pushed into the core's calendar, the people rules run after (`server/friendMap.ts`); its route (`server/routes.ts`) at its own place, `/life-archive/api/m/friend-map`, and at the paths it mounts: friend-map's contract at `/api/archive/` (`server/http.ts`, with the page's scoring, `server/page/scoring.ts`), its page's own API at `/friend-map/api/` (`server/page/pageApi.ts`: settings, view settings, usage, the captain's photo) and the chat a Messages row's drawer opens at `/life-archive/api/content/messages/` (`server/chat.ts`); its page, friend-map's, which the core serves at `/friend-map/`; its rows in the calendar, calls, hangouts and each day's whom-he-messaged summaries (`server/calendarRows.ts`, pushed by `server/calendarPush.ts`), and its answers for them; the Messages, Calls, Hangouts, People and Tags tables (`server/tabs/`), which the Events page's Tables lists, the Calls' and Hangouts' editors (`server/tableEdits.ts`: each edit, and each hangout or logged call added or deleted there, a changeset of its change log; an imported call is never deleted) and the People's and Tags' (`server/peopleEdits.ts`: a person's fields and a tag's name and order, each through People's or the tag table's own write, someone added by hand added and deleted, never anyone from the chats, a tag never a built-in one; the People table's Closeness from `server/tabs/closeness.ts`); its commands (`server/commands.ts`, `server/cli/`), which `npm run archive` hands it; its checks for `npm run check-data` (`server/checkData.ts`); its jobs for the Sources tab (`jobs/sources.ts`); `docs/platforms.md`, written from its connectors' manifests (`server/platformsDoc.ts`); its made-up chats for the testing data (`fixtures/madeUpChats.ts`); and its lines of the privacy policy (`server/privacy.ts`).
- `viewer.tsx`: the Friend Map aspect and its one view: friend-map, the page embedded in a frame filling the area (`viewer/FriendMapView.tsx`, `viewer/frame.ts`: always `/friend-map/` on the console's own origin, in the console's look, at a person's panel when the console's address names one, `?person=`), with no tabs over it. The module's tables are shown and edited on the Events page's Tables alone; the Messages tab this page had is gone, and its old address opens that table there (`moved`). Inside the frame, friend-map's switcher lists its map views alone: its own Table folded into Events' Tables (Hangouts and Calls, then People, its tag manager as Tags), and its own Calendar into Events' Calendar, and their old addresses open those places there (`page/src/demos/movedToEvents.ts`); the Table's Hide and Changes are the live map views' own now, and a tag renamed in Events' Tags keeps its switch in Hide (`saved`, `viewer/hide.ts`).
- `server/dayChats.ts`: the Events calendar's day of chats (under `server/chat.ts`'s mount of the content routes): a day's chats by count, and a person's or a chat's messages that day, read one at a time when opened and kept in the trail.
- `page/`: friend-map's page, its `index.html` and every file it reaches, the module's own code since friend-map-frontend was retired (brought in from its commit 45ec3e6, only what the page served here needs). Its `src/archive.ts` re-exports the core's contract (`sdk/contract.ts`), one copy. Four of its files are over the 1000-line cap, on the ratchet (`oversized-files.json`). It still links to friend-map's public demo (friend-map-demo.pages.dev) and has a Demo entry, until the captain decides whether the demo stays; its copies of the archive's name check and query language are held to the archive's by `server/pageCopies.test.ts`.
- `server/`: the archive's schema and migrations (`db.ts`; `migrated.ts`, what `npm run archive -- migrate` says of them), people and their rules (`people.ts`, `archiving.ts`, `tags.ts`, `locations.ts`, `personFields.ts`, `stats.ts`, `catchUps.ts`, `manualCalls.ts`, `calls.ts`, `drafts.ts`, `addressBookNotes.ts`), its change log over the core's (`changeLog.ts`) and bulk edits (`edits.ts`), the two read paths (`structure.ts` and `content.ts`, with `calendarView.ts` friend-map's Calendar's days), the query language and SQL (`query.ts`, `sqlQuery.ts`, `sqlRead.ts`, `queryWriter.ts`), the sends (`send.ts`, turned off), the exports and media (`platformExports.ts`, `repoint.ts`, `storage.ts`), the repair of the phone export's doubled chats (`messengerPhoneChats.ts`), what must hold before an import (`beforeImport.ts`) and its dry run (`dryRun.ts`).
- `connectors/`: the chat connectors, written as the core's are (`../../sdk/connector.ts`): `messenger-export`, `facebook-dyi`, `instagram-dyi`, `whatsapp-db` (with `macCalls.ts`, `npm run whatsapp-mac-calls`), `imessage-db`, `call-history`, `wechat-ios` (with `extract.ts`, `npm run extract-wechat`), `x-archive`, `signal-export`, `discord-package`, and the live bridges `matrix-bridge` (Telegram) and `matrix-whatsapp`; and what they share (`addressBook.ts`, `appDb.ts`, `iphoneBackup.ts`, `metaDyi.ts`, `metaDyiParts.ts`, `protobuf.ts`).
- `jobs/`: `mac-imports.ts` (`npm run mac-imports`: this Mac's calls and Messages every 15 minutes) and `cua-exports.ts` (`npm run cua-exports`: the exports the Cua Space downloads), each with its launchd agent. The agents installed before the module run the old paths in the core (`server/mac-imports.ts`, `server/cua-exports.ts`, `server/connectors/whatsapp-db/macCalls.ts`), which hand on to these (`server/handOn.ts`) until they are reinstalled; a reinstalled mac-imports launcher is a new program, whose Full Disk Access must be granted again.
- `fixtures/`: its made-up data: the sample Messenger export (`data/messenger/messages.zip`, which `npm run make-fixtures` writes) and six sample people (`data/people.json`), the other platforms' made-up exports and databases the tests build, the bridge rig, and the made-up crowd of `npm run testing-data -- --crowd`.
- `testing/`: what its tests open an archive with (`archive.ts`: a life archive on a testing folder with the module loaded, its friend map's own parts at hand).

**Its data** lives in the archive's folder: the archive, `modules/friend-map/archive.sqlite` (`npm run archive -- backup` keeps a checked copy of it beside the core's, `<stamp>-<label>.archive.sqlite`, and the NAS backup sends one each night); the page's own files, `modules/friend-map/page/` (`settings.json`, `view-settings.json`, `usage.jsonl`, `me/`; friend-map-frontend's `data/` until the switch); the platforms' exports and media in `platform-exports/`; the connectors' settings in `connectors/<id>.json`; and the jobs' state in `mac-imports/` and `whatsapp-mac-calls/`. Message text, media, the captain's notes on hangouts and his drafts are content; people's names, aliases, accounts, tags and locations, and his notes for people, are metadata (`server/privacy.ts`, `docs/privacy.md`).

**Switch it off** with `"modules": {"friend-map": false}` in the archive's `settings.json`: the Friend Map aspect, the four tables, the twelve connectors, the people commands, `/api/archive/` and `/friend-map/` go with it (they answer 404), and the rest of the archive is as it was. While its database cannot be opened (no archive's folder, a broken file), its paths outside `/life-archive/` answer 503, saying why.

**When the module came**, the archive moved rather than being taken over: until then `archive.sqlite` at the folder's root held everything, the core's tables among the chats. `npm run archive -- migrate friend-map-module` (`server/archive/migrate.ts` in the core), with the server stopped, says what it would do; with `--apply` it takes a checked backup (`backups/<stamp>-before-friend-map-module.sqlite`), copies the core's tables into `core.sqlite` with their ids and checks every count, copies the trail of content reads into the calendar when the calendar has it not, moves the archive to `modules/friend-map/archive.sqlite`, and drops from it what is no longer its own (the core's tables, the trail, and exercise's and rituals' rows from before their modules), overwritten for good. Until then the server and the CLI refuse the folder, saying to run it. To undo it, stop the server, delete `core.sqlite` and `modules/friend-map/archive.sqlite`, copy the backup to `archive.sqlite` and start the code from before. A new archive holds none of those tables either: the early migrations in `server/db.ts` still make them, as they did when the archive held everything, and migration 66 drops them while every one is empty, so a new archive and a moved one have the same schema (`server/migrate.test.ts`); an archive whose move is unfinished keeps them, rows and all, for the move.
